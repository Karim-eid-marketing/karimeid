# Concise, evidence-first portfolio

## Goal
Make the existing portfolio faster to scan, more credible, and clearly suited to full-time Influencer Marketing / DTC Growth roles without rebuilding it or removing proof.

## Changes
- Replace long, sales-heavy introductions with short factual statements.
- Update experience to match the supplied roles, dates, employment types, ownership, and results.
- Lead with five defensible numbers: **200% organic growth, 100+ creators, up to 4x ROAS, 30% first-month sales growth, EGP 100K budget**.
- Remove unsupported or conflicting headline metrics and reduce duplicate metric grids.
- Remove “Media Buying” as a label/positioning phrase while retaining factual Paid Media and Meta Ads experience.
- Keep existing case-study screenshots and campaign evidence, but tighten each case study to Challenge → Actions → Results.
- Reduce or remove repetitive sections, process essays, FAQs, and exaggerated sales language.
- Keep the intro video, resume download, contact links, and current visual identity.

## Verification
- Check the full page at desktop and mobile widths for length, readability, and broken layouts.
- Confirm the resume link and primary navigation work.
- Confirm the project builds without errors.

## Technical details
- Consolidate repeated portfolio data and render fewer, stronger sections from the existing React components.
- Preserve all routes and public interfaces; no publishing or backend changes.
